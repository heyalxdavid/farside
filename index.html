Farside We build the ships that leave.

Farside designs, welds and flies reusable starships from a shipyard on the coast. Each flight goes a little farther than the last.

Every ship starts as a stack of steel rings.

Longreach is 52 metres of stainless steel, welded from rings nine metres across in a hangar beside the pad. We finish one every three weeks.

  1. Engine bay

    Six engines burning methane and liquid oxygen. Three small bells for the thick air at sea level, three wide ones for vacuum.

  2. Propellant tanks

    Two tanks that share one domed wall. Together they hold 1,200 tonnes of propellant, chilled to −180 °C.

  3. Heat shield and flaps

    18,000 ceramic tiles on the side that meets the fire on the way home. Four flaps steer the fall, like a skydiver's arms.

  4. Crew deck

    Room for twelve people, a galley, and a row of windows that face home.
